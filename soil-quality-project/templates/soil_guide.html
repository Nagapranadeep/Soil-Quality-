UNDERSTANDING SOIL

Know Your Soil

The machine learning models use twelve soil characteristics to determine fertility.

PRIMARY NUTRIENTS

Major Soil Nutrients

N

Nitrogen

Nitrogen is an important nutrient associated with plant and vegetative growth.

P

Phosphorus

Phosphorus contributes to plant development and root growth.

K

Potassium

Potassium is an important nutrient used when evaluating soil fertility.

SOIL CHARACTERISTICS

Chemical Properties

pH

Soil pH

Indicates the acidity or alkalinity of soil.

EC

Electrical Conductivity

Related to dissolved ions and salts in soil.

OC

Organic Carbon

Represents carbon associated with organic material in soil.

S

Sulphur

Sulphur is another nutrient measured in the dataset.
